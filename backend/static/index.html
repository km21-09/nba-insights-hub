About

NBA Insight Hub is a basketball analytics dashboard that visualizes player performance using real stats and full-court shot charts. You can search players, explore their scoring profile, and see where they take and make shots on a realistic NBA court. Leaderboards let you compare top performers across points, rebounds, assists, and blocks.

Hello, I am Kanav Mittal. I study in Delhi Private School, Dubai in Grade 12, graduating in 2027. Basketball has always been one of the things I connect with the most, the strategy, the numbers, and the stories behind every performance. I’ve always enjoyed breaking things down, understanding patterns, and finding meaning in data, whether it’s through school, personal projects, or just curiosity. Building this app was a way for me to combine that love for analytics with the sport I grew up watching, and to create something that reflects how I see the game: through detail, precision, and genuine passion.
